Who's behind it

A face, not a mailbox.

Valeria Villa

CEO · Villa’s Properties

Valeria Villa grew up in Cernobbio, a village on Lake Como right on the Swiss border, where you learn early on that a house is never just a house: it's the place where someone decides to stay.

She studied Anthropology in Milan, and that way of looking at things has never left her. Before talking about square metres and prices, she understands how a family lives, what brought them here, and what they're really looking for when they say they want “something with sea views”.

A decade ago she joined Century 21, one of the world's largest real estate networks, and there she learned the trade with their method: how to value a property with data rather than intuition, how to negotiate without breaking either side's trust, and how to support a deal through to the notary signing.

Today she runs Villa's Properties in the south of Tenerife, applying both schools at once: the rigour of a major international firm and the outlook of someone who knows that behind every sale there's a move, an inheritance, a divorce, or the start of a new life.

From Lake Como to the Atlantic, she keeps doing the same thing: helping people find their place.
